If your iPhone speaker sounds dull, faint, or muffled after getting wet, liquid is almost certainly trapped against the fine mesh grille. In most cases, the internal speaker hardware is undamaged, but liquid surface tension prevents droplets from draining on their own. Following proper drying procedures and playing a low-frequency ejection tone can quickly dislodge the fluid and restore crisp audio.

Trapped water acts as an acoustic damper. When sound waves leave the speaker driver, water covering the grille absorbs vibrations and muffles high-frequency clarity.

## Quick fix steps

Follow these safe steps to clear trapped water without risking device damage:

1. **Remove cases and covers immediately.** Take off any protective case or skin covering the bottom of your iPhone. Cases trap pools of liquid against the speaker openings and prevent fresh air from reaching the mesh.
2. **Unplug any charging cables.** Disconnect Lightning or USB-C cables right away. Never charge a phone while moisture remains in or around the bottom ports.
3. **Wipe the exterior dry.** Use a clean, lint-free microfiber cloth to dry the exterior glass, frame, and bottom speaker openings. Avoid rough paper towels that can shed wet paper fibers.
4. **Tap the phone gently against your palm.** Hold your iPhone upright with the speaker and charging port facing downward. Gently tap the phone against your hand. [Apple Support guidance](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102643) advises tapping with the connector facing down to remove excess liquid.
5. **Play a low-frequency sound tone.** Point the bottom edge of your phone toward a cloth and play a low-frequency tone at full volume. Our [Fix iPhone Speaker tool](/fix-iphone-speaker) generates a 165Hz tone that physically vibrates the speaker diaphragm back and forth, propelling trapped liquid droplets out through the mesh.
6. **Place the device in an area with cool airflow.** Set your iPhone in a dry room with the bottom speaker facing down. Standing the phone in front of a small fan blowing cool air directly across the ports accelerates evaporation.
7. **Allow time to dry completely.** Apple recommends waiting at least several hours before plugging in a charging cable, and up to 24 hours for complete internal drying if substantial water entered the housing.

## Why it happens

To understand why a wet iPhone sounds distorted, consider how the speaker operates. The bottom speaker consists of an electromagnetic driver attached to a flexible membrane. When playing audio, the membrane vibrates rapidly to push pulses of air through the speaker grille.

Modern iPhones (iPhone 7 and later) feature water-resistant acoustic membranes across the speaker ports, rated [IP67 or IP68 under IEC standard 60529](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108039). These grilles have microscopic pores designed to let air sound waves pass through while blocking liquid water molecules.

However, water has strong surface tension. When liquid contacts the grille, it forms a tight film over the pores. This liquid barrier adds mass to the vibrating diaphragm and dampens sound. As a result, treble and speech consonants sound deadened, bass sounds hollow or buzzing, and total volume drops because air cannot move freely through the mesh.

Water sitting on the exterior mesh does not mean your iPhone is broken. However, if left to evaporate without movement, minerals in tap or pool water can dry into a crust over the mesh holes.

## Common drying mistakes to avoid

Avoid these common mistakes that often cause permanent damage:

- **Do not put your iPhone in rice.** [Apple's official liquid alert documentation](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102643) explicitly warns that rice particles and fine starch dust can enter openings and damage your iPhone.
- **Do not use a hairdryer, heat gun, or oven.** Heat softens water-resistant adhesive seals and damages lithium-ion battery cells.
- **Do not poke the speaker mesh with pins or cotton swabs.** Puncturing the fine protective mesh ruins factory water resistance and risks piercing the speaker diaphragm.
- **Do not blow compressed air into the speaker.** High-pressure air forces trapped water past internal gaskets deeper into the phone.

## When it's real damage (and when to get repairs)

A muffled speaker usually clears up once water is dislodged and evaporated. However, liquid can occasionally cause permanent physical or electrical damage.

Seek repair at an Apple Store or authorized service provider if:

- **Harsh buzzing or rattling persists.** If the speaker buzzes or rattles across all volume levels after 24 hours of drying, the speaker coil or cone may be damaged.
- **No sound comes from the speaker.** If media, alarms, and ringtones produce total silence, an audio amplifier chip may have shorted.
- **Liquid detection alert will not clear.** If your iPhone continually displays a liquid alert after 48 hours in dry airflow, port corrosion may have occurred.
- **Condensation appears under the camera lens.** Fogging inside lenses indicates water breached internal seals.
- **The device becomes abnormally hot.** Heat or sudden battery drain indicates an electrical short that requires immediate service.

Apple notes that [liquid damage is not covered by the standard one-year warranty](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108039). ## Frequently asked questions

### Will trapped water permanently ruin my iPhone speaker?
If fresh water stays on the exterior mesh and the phone seals hold, it rarely causes lasting damage once cleared. However, liquids with salt, chlorine, or sugar can corrode components or leave sticky deposits if not cleaned promptly.

### How long does an iPhone speaker take to dry?
Sound ejection tools dislodge surface droplets in a few minutes. Full evaporation of microscopic moisture in surrounding cavities takes between 1 and 24 hours depending on room ventilation and humidity.

### Can I clean the speaker grille with rubbing alcohol?
Do not pour or squirt alcohol into the speaker openings. Solvents can dissolve the adhesives and rubber gaskets that protect your iPhone from water ingress. Use only a dry, lint-free cloth.

### Why does my speaker still sound low after 24 hours of drying?
If audio remains muffled after a full day, dried minerals may be clogging the exterior mesh, or moisture entered the driver assembly. Inspect the grille under a bright light for visible blockage before visiting a repair technician.
